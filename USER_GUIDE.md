# PulseCut AI — User Guide

Welcome to **PulseCut AI — Intelligent Clipping Studio**, developed by **Code With Bitwizards**.

---

## 1. Overview & Key Capabilities

PulseCut AI transforms long horizontal videos, gaming streams, and commentary into viral, platform-calibrated 9:16 Shorts with zero cloud uploads, zero subscriptions, and zero API keys:

1. **Dual-Source Ingestion:** Drag and drop any local video file (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV) or paste a direct video URL with real-time CORS validation.
2. **AI Highlight Clipping:** Automatically locates high-energy moments and aligns clip boundaries to natural speech pauses.
3. **GTA VI Gaming Clipper:** Dedicated highlight engine detecting action bursts, sirens, gunfire, crashes, and protecting game minimaps / HUD elements.
4. **Find Moments (AI Search):** Search your video for specific dialogue or action using natural-language queries (e.g. *"funny moment"*, *"GTA 6"*).
5. **Kinetic Subtitle Studio:** 12 animated subtitle themes (Karaoke, Creator Punch, Bold Yellow, Neon, Gaming) rendered with word-level highlight synchronization.
6. **60 FPS Hardware Render:** WebCodecs GPU encoding delivering clean MP4 files directly to your device.

---

## 2. Quick-Start Workflow

### Step 1: Ingest Your Video
- Go to `/studio`.
- Choose **Upload Video File** (drag & drop your MP4/MOV) or **Import Video URL** (for direct media links).

### Step 2: Choose Your Clipping Engine
- **AI Clipping:** General dialogue summarization with sentence boundary alignment.
- **GTA VI Gaming:** Highlights gameplay action, vehicle chases, and streamer hype moments while preserving the minimap.
- **Find Moments:** Type a custom query to locate specific moments instantly.

### Step 3: Customize Clip Boundaries & Subtitles
- Click the sliders icon on any candidate clip card.
- Adjust start and end times, choose your aspect ratio (`9:16`, `1:1`, `16:9`, `4:5`), edit hook headlines, and select your favorite subtitle typography.

### Step 4: Render & Export
- Click **Render** on any clip or **Render All** to batch-process all shorts.
- Download finished 60 FPS MP4 files with sound preserved.
